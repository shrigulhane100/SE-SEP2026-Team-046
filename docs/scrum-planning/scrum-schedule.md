## 2. Pre-Sprint – Milestone 2

**Objective:** Organise the project team, establish the technical direction and prepare the project for development.

Three team meetings form the basis of the M2 planning activities.

### 2.1 Pre-Sprint Meeting Schedule

| Meeting | Proposed date | Topic | Main participants | Expected output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meeting 1 | 30 October 2026 | Project Discussion | All five team members | Shared understanding of project concept, scope and deliverables |
| Meeting 2 | 31 October 2026 | Roles and Responsibilities | All five team members | Defined team roles, task ownership and responsibilities |
| Meeting 3 | 2 November 2026 | Technical and Project Planning | All five team members | API/frontend plan, design direction, charts and Scrum schedule |

### 2.2 Meeting 1 – Project Discussion

**Agenda:**
- Discuss the Rock Starz project concept and proposed solution.
- Review the user requirements identified in M1.
- Discuss the problem statement, project objectives and scope.
- Identify major platform components.
- Discuss expected milestone deliverables.

**Expected outcome:** Agreement on the project direction, scope and major areas of development.

### 2.3 Meeting 2 – Roles and Responsibilities

**Agenda:**
- Identify the responsibilities required for project execution.
- Discuss individual strengths and areas of contribution.
- Allocate project management, frontend, backend, testing and technical review responsibilities.
- Establish individual ownership of tasks.
- Discuss team coordination and communication.

**Expected outcome:** Defined team responsibilities and ownership of project activities.

### 2.4 Meeting 3 – Technical and Project Planning

**Agenda:**
- Discuss frontend structure and UI implementation.
- Plan backend APIs and frontend-backend communication.
- Discuss component architecture, UML class diagram and database design.
- Plan Gantt chart and Kanban board configuration.
- Establish the Scrum planning approach and sprint schedule.
- Discuss the development workflow, Git branches and Pull Requests.

**Expected outcome:** Initial technical direction, design plans and project execution schedule.

## 6. Recurring Meeting Structure

| Meeting type | Proposed frequency | Duration | Participants |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint Planning | Once per sprint | 45–60 minutes | All team members |
| Progress Meeting | Twice weekly | 15–30 minutes | All team members |
| Sprint Review | Once per sprint | 30–45 minutes | All team members |
| Sprint Retrospective | Once per sprint | 20–30 minutes | All team members |
| Technical Review | Before milestone submission | As required | Shriyash and relevant contributors |

### Standard Progress Meeting Agenda

1. What work has been completed since the previous meeting?
2. What work is currently in progress?
3. Are there any blockers or dependencies?
4. What work is planned before the next meeting?
5. Do GitHub Issues and Project statuses need updating?

## 7. Schedule and Progress Tracking

- GitHub Projects is used as the central task-tracking board.
- GitHub Issues define individual activities, assignments and acceptance criteria.
- Target dates and sprint assignments are maintained in the project board.
- Progress meetings are used to identify blockers and review dependencies.
- Technical review takes place before milestone submission.
- Dates may be revised based on actual progress and team availability.

### 8.1 Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Completed | Meeting or activity has taken place and its outcome has been recorded. |
| In Progress | Activity has started but is not yet complete. |
| Planned | Activity is scheduled but has not started. |
| To Update | Proposed record requiring confirmation of actual date, attendance, duration and outcome. |
| Rescheduled | Original date has changed and a revised date must be recorded. |
| Cancelled | Meeting or activity did not take place. |

### 8.2 Meeting Record Maintenance

- Update tentative dates with actual dates once confirmed.
- Record only members who attended each meeting.
- Update duration based on the actual meeting.
- For completed meetings, replace the planned summary with a concise record of the discussion and decisions.
- Update the status after the meeting or activity.
- Rescheduled meetings should retain their original ID, with the revised date recorded in the Date column.
- GitHub Projects remains the source of truth for individual task progress and assignments.
