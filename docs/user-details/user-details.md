# Rock Starz – User Details

## 1. Overview

Rock Starz is a Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform intended to connect students with mentors whose experience and expertise align with their academic, professional and entrepreneurial goals.

This document carries forward the user-requirement findings established during Project Milestone 1. It records the identified user groups, user stories, potential pain points, requirements insights, assumptions and open questions that will inform the platform's design and implementation.

## 2. User Identification

The platform's users are classified into three groups.

### 2.1 Primary Users – Students

| User type | Description |
|---|---|
| Placements | Students seeking guidance on job opportunities, recruitment, interviews and industry expectations. |
| Higher Studies | Students exploring postgraduate education, research and further academic opportunities. |
| Entrepreneurship | Students interested in starting businesses or developing entrepreneurial ventures. |
| Students Lacking Career Clarity | Students uncertain about their career goals who need help exploring suitable pathways. |

### 2.2 Secondary Users – Mentors

| User type | Description |
|---|---|
| Recent Graduates | Recent graduates sharing their transition experiences and practical career guidance. |
| Industry-Experienced Alumni and Company Representatives | Professionals providing industry insights, job-related guidance and workplace knowledge. |
| Higher-Studies and Research Mentors | Experienced individuals guiding students on higher education, research and academic careers. |
| Entrepreneurial Alumni | Entrepreneurs sharing startup experiences, business insights and venture-building guidance. |
| Faculty Mentors | Faculty members providing academic, research and career guidance based on their expertise. |

### 2.3 Tertiary Users – Institutional and Academic Stakeholders

| User type | Description |
|---|---|
| Campus Placement Team | Uses aggregate career-interest and mentoring insights to identify student support needs and plan placement initiatives. |
| Program Administration and College Team | Oversees mentoring activities, monitors participation and evaluates programme effectiveness. |
| Faculty Members and TAs | Help identify common student guidance needs and direct students towards appropriate academic or career support. |

## 3. User Stories

The following user stories were identified during Milestone 1.

| User group | User story |
|---|---|
| Student – Placements | As a student preparing for placements, I want to find mentors with experience in my preferred industry and job role within 10 minutes so that I can prepare for recruitment and interviews effectively. |
| Student – Placements | As a student who has received an interview opportunity, I want to connect with a mentor experienced in technical interviews within 2 days of submitting a request so that I can prepare for the specific selection process. |
| Student – Higher Studies | As a student planning postgraduate studies, I want to find mentors with relevant academic and research experience within 10 minutes so that I can understand suitable programmes and admission requirements. |
| Student – Higher Studies | As a student considering research opportunities, I want to connect with a mentor experienced in research within 2 days of submitting a request so that I can understand research pathways and preparation requirements. |
| Student – Entrepreneurship | As a student interested in starting a venture, I want to find entrepreneurial alumni with relevant experience within 10 minutes so that I can understand the practical challenges of starting a business. |
| Student – Career Clarity | As a student uncertain about my career direction, I want to explore mentors from different career fields and shortlist at least two within 15 minutes so that I can compare career options based on relevant experience. |
| Student – Career Clarity | As a student unsure about my strengths and interests, I want to discuss my interests and career concerns with a suitable mentor within 2 weeks of requesting guidance so that I can identify career paths worth exploring. |
| Student – Career Clarity | As a student considering a career change, I want to connect with mentors experienced in alternative career paths within 2 weeks so that I can evaluate alternative opportunities before deciding. |
| Recent Graduate Mentor | As a recent graduate serving as a mentor, I want to create a profile describing my career journey and expertise within 10 minutes so that students can identify and request relevant guidance. |
| Industry Mentor | As an industry-experienced mentor, I want to specify my industry, job role and expertise in my profile so that students can find guidance relevant to their career interests. |
| Higher-Studies or Research Mentor | As a higher-studies or research mentor, I want to list my academic background and research interests in my profile so that students can find appropriate academic guidance. |
| Entrepreneurial Alumni | As an entrepreneurial alumnus, I want to describe my venture experience and mentoring areas in my profile so that aspiring entrepreneurs can approach me for relevant advice. |
| Faculty Mentor | As a faculty mentor, I want to specify my academic expertise and research interests in my profile so that students can seek guidance relevant to their academic goals. |
| Campus Placement Team | As a placement team member, I want to view aggregate student career interests and mentoring participation for a selected term so that I can identify common career-support needs. |
| Program Administrator | As a program administrator, I want to view student participation, mentor participation and request statuses for a selected period so that I can monitor the mentoring programme. |
| Faculty Member or TA | As a faculty member or TA, I want to view authorised summaries of common student guidance needs for a selected term so that I can direct students towards appropriate academic or career support. |

## 4. Identified Pain Points

The following pain points were identified as potential challenges and were intended to guide further requirements clarification.

### 4.1 Primary Users – Students

- **PP-01: Difficulty Finding Relevant Mentors** – Students may find it challenging to identify mentors whose expertise matches their career interests and goals.
- **PP-02: Uncertainty About Career Direction** – Students may need guidance to evaluate career options, understand opportunities and identify suitable next steps.
- **PP-03: Geographical Barriers to Mentoring** – Students distributed across India may need accessible remote mentoring opportunities regardless of location.

### 4.2 Secondary Users – Mentors

- **PP-04: Matching Expertise With Student Needs** – Mentors may lack sufficient information about students' goals to determine whether their expertise is suitable.
- **PP-05: Time Constraints and Request Management** – Professional and academic commitments may make it difficult for mentors to manage requests and respond consistently.
- **PP-06: Maintaining Mentoring Continuity** – Tracking mentoring goals, previous discussions and follow-up activities may be challenging without organised records.

### 4.3 Tertiary Users – Institutional and Academic Stakeholders

- **PP-07: Coordinating Career Support** – Coordinating placement, higher-studies, research and entrepreneurship guidance across stakeholders may require an organised process.
- **PP-08: Monitoring Mentoring Activities** – Tracking participation, request statuses and ongoing mentoring relationships may be difficult when information is scattered.
- **PP-09: Evaluating Programme Effectiveness** – Participation counts alone may not indicate whether mentoring meets students' needs. Appropriate feedback and progress indicators may be required.

## 5. Requirements Clarification and Research Insights

Milestone 1 discussions and research highlighted the following considerations for the proposed platform.

### 5.1 Experience-Based Career Guidance

Students may be uncertain about whether to pursue employment, higher studies, research, entrepreneurship or another career path. The platform should allow students to specify the guidance they seek and provide mentor profiles containing career journeys, experience, skills and areas of expertise.

### 5.2 Peer and Alumni Support

Students may benefit from guidance from people who have faced similar academic or professional challenges. The platform should support structured mentoring requests, mentor acceptance or rejection, and ongoing mentoring relationships.

### 5.3 Practical Career Development

Mentoring may involve résumé reviews, project discussions, internship-search guidance, interview preparation, skill planning and general career advice. Mentor discovery should consider relevant industry, roles, internships, research and professional experience.

### 5.4 Continuity in Mentoring

The platform should support both one-time interactions and longer-term mentoring relationships. Relevant goals, interaction history, activity records and relationship statuses may help students and mentors maintain continuity.

### 5.5 Existing IIT Madras Support Ecosystem

The IIT Madras BS programme already provides student-support mechanisms, including peer mentoring, academic discussions, study circles, career guidance, student-led activities, an official Mentor–Mentee Portal and an Alumni Network.

The proposed platform should complement existing facilities rather than assume that mentoring infrastructure is entirely absent.

### 5.6 Career Pathway Diversity

Research into IITM BS student achievements and recruitment-related information highlighted varied pathways involving internships, employment, postgraduate admissions, research, hackathons and entrepreneurship. These findings support exploring mentor discovery based on students' different career objectives.

These findings provide context for the proposed platform; they do not independently establish mentor availability, confirmed hiring outcomes or measurable improvements in placement results.

## 6. Assumptions

The following assumptions were recorded during Milestone 1 and require validation during further requirements analysis.

1. Students will specify their mentoring goals and interests to support relevant mentor recommendations.
2. Mentors will provide accurate information about their experience and expertise.
3. Mentoring may be either short-term or long-term, depending on student requirements.
4. A mentor recommendation does not guarantee a successful mentoring relationship.
5. Mentors should be able to reject requests or limit their availability.
6. The platform will support existing mentoring systems rather than replace them.
7. Only necessary mentoring activity should be tracked; this does not imply that administrators should access private conversations.

## 7. Open Questions and Edge Cases

The following cases were identified for further clarification:

- **Multiple career interests:** Should students receive one mentor recommendation or several recommendations for different interests?
- **Best-matching mentor unavailable:** How should the platform handle a suitable mentor who does not have capacity?
- **Excessive mentor requests:** Should the platform introduce mentee limits, waiting lists or alternative recommendations?
- **Unanswered requests:** Should requests expire after a defined period?
- **One-time interaction:** How should the platform distinguish a single mentoring session from a longer relationship?
- **Inactive mentor:** Should an accepted mentoring relationship be paused, closed or reassigned if a mentor becomes unavailable?
- **Student hesitation:** How can the request process be made simple and less intimidating?
- **Different expectations:** How should the platform clarify that mentoring provides guidance, not guaranteed referrals, project solutions or placements?
- **Unsuitable advice:** Would feedback, reporting or moderation mechanisms be necessary?
- **Changing student workload:** Should students be able to pause and resume mentoring relationships?
- **Perception of an online degree:** Should students be able to seek guidance on presenting their online degree, skills and experience to employers?

## 8. Summary

The Milestone 1 analysis identified three broad user groups: students seeking guidance, mentors providing academic or professional experience, and institutional stakeholders overseeing mentoring activities.

The proposed platform is intended to address relevant mentor discovery, structured mentoring requests, career guidance and continuity of mentoring relationships while complementing existing IIT Madras student-support facilities.

The user categories, stories, pain points, assumptions and open questions documented here provide the requirements foundation for the subsequent design and development activities of Rock Starz.
